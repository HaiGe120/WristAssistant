Wrist Assistant — Support

Wrist Assistant is a private, bring-your-own-key AI chat client for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Contact

Email: you@example.com. GitHub issues: open one.

Privacy

Wrist Assistant does not collect analytics or telemetry. API keys live in the iOS Keychain; conversations live in a local SwiftData store; the only network calls are to the endpoints you configure. iCloud backup is opt-in and uses your private iCloud Drive container.

FAQ

Which providers are supported?

Any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoint — OpenAI, Anthropic, MiniMax, Open WebUI, or your own gateway. You bring the API key.

Does Wrist Assistant see my conversations?

No. Everything stays on your devices and the endpoints you configure.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, your Apple Watch, and (if you opt in) your iCloud Drive. No data is sent to Wrist Assistant because there is no Wrist Assistant server.
